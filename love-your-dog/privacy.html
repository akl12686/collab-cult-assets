Privacy Policy

Love Your Dog Poster app, for the Love Your Dog Facebook Page. Last updated October 14, 2026.

What this app is

Love Your Dog Poster is a private publishing tool. Its only job is to publish and schedule posts, photos and reels on the Love Your Dog Facebook Page (facebook.com/YourDog.info), which we own and manage. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can log in to it.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store or process personal information about Facebook users or visitors to the Page. It does not read comments, messages or profiles, and it does not use cookies or tracking.

The only data the app handles is our own: the content we publish and the access token that lets the app post to our Page. That token is stored privately on our own computer and is never shared.

How information is used and shared

We don't sell, rent or share any data through this app. Nothing is passed to advertisers or other third parties.

Data deletion

Because the app stores no data about you, there is nothing to delete. If you have a question or want to make a request, send a message to the Love Your Dog Page on Facebook (facebook.com/YourDog.info) and we'll respond.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
